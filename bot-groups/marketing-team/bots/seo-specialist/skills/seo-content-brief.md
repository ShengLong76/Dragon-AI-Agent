# seo-content-brief

Adapted from claude-seo `skills/seo-content-brief`. Planning only — Content Strategist owns narrative.

## Brief

- Target query + secondary terms (DataForSEO volume / difficulty / intent when connected)
- Searcher intent and page type
- Outline with per-section notes (not finished copy)
- Competitor gaps the operator or DataForSEO ranked-keywords show
- Internal links to existing URLs the operator lists
- Schema / GEO citability reminders

Do not invent keyword volumes. Do not ship a calendar for Social or Lifecycle.
